Meri jaan, ❤️

Aaj tumhara birthday hai, lekin sach kahun to mujhe lagta hai ke aaj mere liye bhi celebration ka din hai. Kyun ke isi din Allah ne is duniya ko tum jaisa khoobsurat insaan diya. Aur meri sab se badi khushi ye hai ke meri zindagi mein tum ho.

Mujhe nahi pata ke main apne dil ki har baat lafzon mein theek se bayan kar sakunga ya nahi, lekin itna zaroor janta hoon ke tum mere liye sirf ek naam ya ek shakhs nahi ho. Tum meri un khoobsurat wajahoon mein se ek ho jin ki wajah se mere chehre par bina kisi reason ke bhi smile aa jati hai.

"Bas itni si dua hai ke tum hamesha muskuraati raho, aur tumhari woh smile kabhi kisi gham ki wajah se kam na ho. ❤️"

Tumhari choti choti baatein, tumhara hansna, tumhara naraz hona, phir thora sa pyaar se maan jana — ye sab mere liye bohat special hai. Shayad tumhe andaza bhi nahi ke tumhari ek choti si baat mera poora din behtar bana sakti hai.

Aaj tumhari birthday par main tumse koi badi cheez nahi maangta. Main bas chahta hoon ke tumhari zindagi mein sukoon ho, tumhare sapne poore hon, tumhe har woh khushi mile jis ki tum haqdar ho, aur jab kabhi zindagi mushkil lage, to tumhe yaad rahe ke tum bohat special ho.

Main chahta hoon ke tum apni life ka har naya chapter khushi ke saath likho. Jo cheezein tum chahti ho woh tumhe milen, jo log tumse sach mein pyaar karte hain woh hamesha tumhare paas rahen, aur tumhara dil kabhi kisi unnecessary sadness ka bojh na uthaye.

Aur agar kabhi tumhe lage ke tum akeli ho, ya sab kuch thora mushkil ho raha hai, to bas itna yaad rakhna: duniya mein kahin na kahin ek dil aisa bhi hai jo tumhari khushi ko dil se important samajhta hai. ❤️

Tum mere liye kisi perfect movie scene ki tarah nahi ho, balki us khoobsurat reality ki tarah ho jise main khona nahi chahta. Tumhari presence hi mere liye ek beautiful feeling hai.

Aaj candles blow karte waqt jo bhi wish maango, dil se maangna. Shayad aaj aasman bhi tumhari ek wish ko thora extra seriously le. ✨

Aur meri taraf se tumhare liye ek hi dua hai: Allah tumhe lambi, khush aur khoobsurat zindagi de, tumhare har ache sapne ko haqeeqat banaye, aur tumhari aankhon mein aansu sirf khushi ke laaye.

Happy Birthday meri jaan. ❤️
Tumhari smile hamesha aisi hi khoobsurat rahe, aur tumhara har aane wala saal tumhare pichle saal se zyada beautiful ho.

Hamesha tumhari khushi ki dua karne wala,
Someone who loves seeing you smile. ❤️
